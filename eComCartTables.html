OUR STORY & CRAFT

Born from a passion for monolithic forms.

eComCarttables.com was founded on a singular architectural conviction: contemporary spaces deserve furniture that feels grounded, authentic, and deliberate. Instead of mass-produced veneer boards, we revere the organic weight of solid wood, hand-sculpted travertine, and structural blackened steel.

Operating directly from our workshops in India, we work alongside architects, interior curators, and discerning homeowners across the nation. Every design in our catalogue is adaptable: whether you require an extended 10-seater dining proportion or a customized stone slab, our team accommodates your exact spatial specifications.
